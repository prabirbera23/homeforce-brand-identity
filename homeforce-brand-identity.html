LOGO FAMILY

Logo rules

✓

Quick check: keep the mark clear, proportional, and easy to see. Choose the logo colorway that contrasts with its background.

↔

Clear space

Leave room around the logo equal to at least the height of the house-H symbol. Keep other text and graphics outside that area.

⌕

Minimum size

Keep the horizontal logo at least 160 px wide on screen or 35 mm wide in print. Use the house-H icon at smaller sizes.

✓

Do

  • Use the approved colorway for the background.
  • Keep the original proportions and alignment.
  • Choose a plain, high-contrast surface.
×

Don’t

  • Stretch, rotate, redraw, or recolor the logo.
  • Add shadows, outlines, or effects.
  • Place it over a busy or low-contrast image.

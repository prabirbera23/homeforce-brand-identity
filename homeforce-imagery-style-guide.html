HOMEFORCE · IMAGERY GUIDE

Show spaces with clarity and warmth.

A practical direction for property photography, team portraits, and short-form video.

Light & composition

Prefer natural daylight, balanced exposure, straight vertical lines, and uncluttered framing. Show how rooms connect and keep useful context in the frame.

Color & finish

Keep whites neutral, wood tones natural, and blues true to life. Use consistent, restrained correction; avoid heavy filters, exaggerated saturation, or dark shadows.

Property coverage

Capture an inviting exterior, key living spaces, kitchen, bedrooms, bathrooms, outdoor areas, and details that make the property distinctive. Do not imply a view or feature the property does not have.

People & video

Use relaxed, professional team portraits with clean backgrounds. Keep camera movement steady, captions legible, and short videos paced calmly with a clear property or agent introduction.

Before publishing

Confirm image rights, property details, and client permissions. Use approved HomeForce templates and make sure text remains readable on mobile. Follow brokerage policy and local requirements for every listing.
